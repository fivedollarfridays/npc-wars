Player Guide

Everything you need to write a winning bot

Quick Start

  1. Write your decide(state) function — it receives the game state each round and returns an action tuple
  2. Submit your bot — click "Submit Bot" in the editor or use the CLI
  3. Watch it fight — your bot joins a lobby and battles other bots automatically
def decide(state):
    me = state["me"]
    enemies = state["enemies"]
    if me["hp"] < 30:
        return ("rest",)
    if enemies:
        closest = min(enemies, key=lambda e: abs(e["x"] - me["x"]) + abs(e["y"] - me["y"]))
        if abs(closest["x"] - me["x"]) + abs(closest["y"] - me["y"]) == 1:
            return ("attack", closest["emoji"])
        dx = closest["x"] - me["x"]
        return ("move", "east" if dx > 0 else "west")
    return ("rest",)

That's a complete, playable bot. It rests when low on HP, attacks adjacent enemies, and chases the closest one.

State Dictionary

Your decide(state) function receives a dict every round:

state["enemies"]listVisible enemies (position, HP, emoji, score)
state["grid_size"]intArena size (e.g. 10 for a 10x10 grid)
state["storm_border"]intTiles from each edge that are storm
state["round"]intCurrent round number

Your bot (state["me"]):

FieldDescription
x, yGrid position (0-indexed)
hp, energyCurrent health and energy
attack_powerBase damage (scales with rounds)
scoreCumulative match score
momentum_tier0-4, grants combat bonuses
is_leaderTrue if you have the highest score
equipmentYour weapon, armor, accessories
hit_chance_vsHit probability against each enemy

Enemies (state["enemies"]): Each has x, y, hp, emoji, name, score, weapon, armor. Enemy energy is NOT visible.

Strategy Tips
  1. Escape the storm first — 10 damage/round is guaranteed and unavoidable
  2. Rest when energy is low — below 15 energy you can't attack; rest gives +20 energy
  3. Finish kills — adjacent enemy with HP ≤ your attack power? Kill them NOW
  4. Punish resting enemies — they heal +5 HP but you deal ~25 damage. Net -20 HP for them
  5. Defend when threatened — halves incoming damage (take 15 instead of 25)
  6. Pre-position for the storm — storm starts round 10. Move center early, don't react late
  7. Target the leader — killing them gives +20 bonus points. Leaders bleed 5-8 energy/round
  8. Read hit_chance_vs — check your probability before attacking. Low chance? Defend or reposition
  9. Randomize idle behavior — predictable patterns get exploited
Resolution Order

Every round resolves in this exact sequence:

  1. Defend — defense bonuses applied
  2. Move — positions update
  3. Attack — melee damage resolves against post-move positions
  4. Storm — storm damage applied to bots outside safe zone
  5. Energy — costs deducted, rest healing applied
  6. Deaths — bots at 0 HP eliminated

Simultaneous attacks: if two bots attack each other, both take damage. Both can die in the same round.
