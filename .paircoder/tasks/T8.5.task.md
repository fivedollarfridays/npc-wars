# Objective

Award +30 energy on kill to reward aggression and enable kill chains.

# Implementation Plan

1. Create `tests/test_kill_bounty.py`: test combat kill awards 30 energy, storm kill no bounty, energy capped at MAX_ENERGY
2. Add `KILL_BOUNTY_ENERGY = 30` to `engine/combat.py`
3. In `engine/rounds.py` `attribute_kills()`, after incrementing `killer.kills`: add `killer.energy = min(killer.energy + KILL_BOUNTY_ENERGY, MAX_ENERGY)` when killer is not "storm"/"unknown"

# Acceptance Criteria

- [x] KILL_BOUNTY_ENERGY == 30
- [x] Killer bot receives +30 energy on combat kill
- [x] Storm kills do NOT award bounty
- [x] Energy capped at MAX_ENERGY (100) after bounty
- [x] Kill bounty applied in same round as kill attribution
