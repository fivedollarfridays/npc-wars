# Objective

Add `dash` action -- move 2 tiles in the specified direction, bump anything in the path, costs 15 energy. Dash synergizes with bumper physics (T8.6) for chain reactions. Reference: docs/RESEARCH-spectacle-and-human-play.md Section 9.

# Implementation Plan

1. **Create `tests/test_dash.py`** with tests for:
   - `("dash", "east")` moves bot 2 tiles east
   - Bumps a bot standing at tile 1 (intermediate)
   - Bumps a bot standing at tile 2 (destination)
   - Costs 15 energy (bot must have >= 15 energy)
   - Stops at grid edge (doesn't wrap or go out of bounds)
   - Chain reactions triggered via `resolve_bumps` for each intermediate tile

2. **Add constant to `engine/combat.py`**:
   - `DASH_COST = 15`

3. **Update `engine/sandbox.py` VALID_ACTIONS**:
   - Add `"dash": 1` (takes 1 argument: direction)

4. **Handle dash in `resolve_movement` (engine/rounds.py)**:
   - Move bot 2 tiles in the specified direction
   - For each intermediate tile, check occupancy and trigger bumps via `resolve_bumps`
   - Stop at grid edge if fewer than 2 tiles remain in that direction

# Acceptance Criteria

- [x] ("dash", "east") moves bot 2 tiles east
- [x] Bumps any bot in the path (tile 1 and tile 2)
- [x] Costs 15 energy
- [x] Stops at grid edge (doesn't wrap or go OOB)
- [x] Integrates with bumper physics for chain reactions
