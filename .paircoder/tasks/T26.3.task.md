# Objective

Killing the leader (the tier 3+ bot) gives the killer a massive score bonus — enough for an instant tier jump. This makes high-momentum bots worth targeting and creates organic "boss fight" moments.

# Bounty Rules

- **Leader bounty**: Killing the leader gives +20 bonus points to the killer
- **Non-leader kill**: Normal +10 (unchanged)
- **Storm kills leader**: No bounty awarded (no killer)
- **Leader bounty event**: `{"type": "leader_bounty", "killer": emoji, "victim": emoji, "bonus": 20}`
- **Visible to all**: `state["enemies"][i]["is_leader"]` already shows who to target (from T26.1)

# Implementation Plan

1. **Update `calculate_round_scores()` in `engine/scoring.py`**:
   - Accept `leader_emoji: str | None` parameter
   - When a kill event's victim is the leader and attacker is not "storm"/"unknown":
     - Award +20 bonus to the killer (on top of normal +10 kill)
     - Add `ScoreEvent(killer, "leader_bounty", 20)`

2. **Update `_apply_round_scores()` in `engine/game.py`**:
   - Pass `leader_emoji` to `calculate_round_scores()`
   - The leader is determined before scoring (from T26.1's `determine_leader()`)

3. **Add leader bounty to kill feed** (`agentgrounds/wars/cli/feed.py`):
   - New formatter for `leader_bounty` event type
   - `R15: 🤖 👑 REGICIDE! +20pts for killing leader 🐢`

4. **Tests** in `tests/test_leader_bounty.py`:
   - Killing leader: killer gets 10 (kill) + 20 (bounty) = 30 points
   - Killing non-leader: normal 10 points
   - Storm kills leader: no bounty
   - Leader bounty event in round data
   - Clean kill bonus stacks: 10 + 20 + 5 = 35 points

# Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Killing leader awards +20 bonus points
- [ ] Non-leader kills unchanged (+10)
- [ ] Storm kills don't award bounty
- [ ] Leader bounty event recorded in round data
- [ ] Kill feed shows REGICIDE message
- [ ] Tests pass, ruff clean
