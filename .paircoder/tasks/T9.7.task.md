# Objective

Add `taunt` action -- forces all bots within Manhattan distance 2 to target the taunter next round. Costs 10 energy. Enables tank/support play patterns. Reference: docs/RESEARCH-spectacle-and-human-play.md Section 9.

# Implementation Plan

1. **Create `tests/test_taunt.py`** with tests for:
   - `("taunt",)` is a valid action (no arguments)
   - All bots within Manhattan distance 2 get taunted
   - Bots outside Manhattan distance 2 are unaffected
   - Costs 10 energy
   - Taunted bots' next attack is directed at the taunter
   - Taunt effect lasts exactly 1 round (cleared after resolution)

2. **Add constants to `engine/combat.py`**:
   - `TAUNT_COST = 10`
   - `TAUNT_RANGE = 2`

3. **Update `engine/sandbox.py` VALID_ACTIONS**:
   - Add `"taunt": 0` (takes no arguments)

4. **Add `resolve_taunt()` to `engine/rounds.py`**:
   - Find all bots within Manhattan distance `TAUNT_RANGE` of the taunter
   - Mark affected bots with `taunt_target` attribute (set to taunter's identifier)
   - Effect persists for next round only

5. **In `resolve_decisions` (engine/rounds.py)**:
   - If a bot has `taunt_target` set, override their attack direction toward the taunter
   - Clear `taunt_target` after the round resolves

# Acceptance Criteria

- [x] Costs 10 energy
