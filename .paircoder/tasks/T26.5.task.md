# Objective

Integration gate for S26. Verify the king-of-the-hill mechanics work end-to-end. Run balance simulation to confirm energy drain prevents runaway winners.

# Implementation Plan

1. **Integration test** `tests/test_s26_integration.py`:
   - Run a real match, verify only one bot has `is_leader=True` at any round
   - Verify non-leaders are capped at tier 2 in final stats
   - Verify leader has tier 3 or 4 in final stats
   - Verify energy drain: leader's energy is lower than expected from rest alone
   - Verify killing leader awards bounty: check score events for `leader_bounty` type
   - Verify `state["me"]["is_leader"]` is in position data
   - Verify roster output contains 👑 for leader
   - Verify kill feed contains "REGICIDE" when leader is killed
   - Verify PROMPT.md mentions "leader" and "bounty"

2. **Balance simulation**:
   - Run 100 matches with `agentgrounds wars sim --matches 100 --seed 42`
   - Compare with S25 baseline (48% max win rate)
   - With energy drain, expect:
     - More balanced win distribution (drain weakens leaders)
     - Slightly shorter matches (energy pressure accelerates endgame)
     - No bot > 60% win rate
   - Document results

3. **Update PROMPT.md**:
   - Add "King of the Hill" section explaining leader mechanics
   - Document energy drain costs
   - Strategy tips: "Target the 👑 leader for +20 bounty points"

4. **Full regression check**
