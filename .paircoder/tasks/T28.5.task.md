# Objective

Replace flat RANGED_ATTACK_DAMAGE=15 in resolve_ranged_attacks() with roll-based damage. Add ranged penalty (-2 to-hit) and reduced damage range (60% of power scaling).

# Design

- Add `roll_ranged_attack()` to combat_rolls.py OR use roll_attack with ranged modifier
- Ranged to-hit penalty: -2 (constant RANGED_HIT_PENALTY)
- Ranged damage scaling: 60% of melee (RANGED_DAMAGE_SCALE = 0.6)
- At default: min_ranged = 9 (15*0.6), max_ranged = 21 (35*0.6), avg = 15 (matches current)
- Add `rng: random.Random` parameter to resolve_ranged_attacks()

# Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Ranged uses roll system with -2 to-hit penalty
- [ ] Ranged damage is 60% of melee damage range
- [ ] Default avg ranged damage = ~15
- [ ] Ranged miss events emitted
- [ ] Tests pass, ruff clean
