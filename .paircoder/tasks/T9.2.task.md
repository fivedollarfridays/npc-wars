# Objective

Track line budget progression based on wins and streaks. The line budget starts at 50 and grows as players win matches and maintain streaks, capped at 200. Beating The Watcher awards a bonus. Reference: docs/RESEARCH-spectacle-and-human-play.md Section 8.

# Implementation Plan

1. **Create `tests/test_line_budget.py`** with tests for:
   - New profile has `line_budget == 50`
   - Single win awards +10 lines
   - 3-win streak awards additional +15 lines
   - 5-win streak awards additional +25 lines
   - 10-win streak awards additional +50 lines
   - Budget never exceeds 200 (hard cap)
   - Beating The Watcher awards +20 lines

2. **Add constants to `data/player_profiles.py`**:
   - `BUDGET_BASE = 50`
   - `BUDGET_CAP = 200`
   - `BUDGET_REWARDS = {1: 10, 3: 15, 5: 25, 10: 50}` (win count thresholds -> line rewards)
   - `BUDGET_WATCHER_BONUS = 20`

3. **Add functions to `data/player_profiles.py`**:
   - `calculate_line_budget(profile: PlayerProfile) -> int` -- sum base + accumulated rewards from wins, streaks, and watcher wins; clamp to BUDGET_CAP
   - `update_after_match(profile: PlayerProfile, won: bool, beat_watcher: bool) -> None` -- update wins, streak, watcher stats, recalculate and set line_budget
