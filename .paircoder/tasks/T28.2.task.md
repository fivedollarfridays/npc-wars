# Objective

Add critical hit mechanics to roll_attack(). Natural 20 always crits. High rolls against low AC also crit. Crit damage multiplied by crit_multiplier from DerivedStats.

# Design

- Crit condition: natural d20 roll == 20, OR total roll >= AC + 8
- Crit damage: `int(base_damage * attacker.crit_multiplier)`
- Default crit_multiplier = 1.5 (from POWER=25)
- High POWER (50) → crit_multiplier = 2.0 → crits hit for double
- Set is_crit=True on CombatResult
- Expected crit rate at default: ~10% (nat 20 = 5%, plus some high rolls vs low AC)

# Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Natural 20 always crits
- [ ] Roll >= AC + 8 crits
- [ ] Crit damage = base * crit_multiplier
